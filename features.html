Work

Turn big assignments and projects into small steps you can actually finish. ARC would show what is due today and what is coming up next.

Planned features

  • Split large tasks into clear, ordered steps
  • Set deadlines with a calendar connection
  • Track time spent to plan better next time

Fitness

Plan your training week, log each session, and see your consistency build up over time.

Planned features

  • Schedule workouts around school and work
  • Track training habits and consistency
  • Look back at progress week by week

Finances

Keep bills, budgets and savings goals together so nothing sneaks up on you.

Planned features

  • Plan ahead for upcoming bills
  • Break savings goals into monthly check-ins
  • Review spending over time

Learning

Classes, test prep, online courses or a new skill. Build a study plan and stick to it.

Planned features

  • Build study schedules with milestones
  • Track time spent on each subject
  • Check off chapters, modules or lessons

Personal

The everyday stuff: errands, habits, appointments and the things you keep meaning to get to.

Planned features

  • Build daily habits
  • Organize errands and appointments
  • Make time for personal goals

Relationships

Stay close to the people who matter, even when life gets busy.

Planned features

  • Remember birthdays and important dates
  • Plan time with family and friends
  • Keep track of things you want to follow up on

Projects

Group projects and side projects could have a shared workspace so everyone can see who is doing what.

Planned features

  • Share a workspace with other members
  • Assign tasks to each team member
  • See progress toward a shared goal

Custom

Doesn't fit anywhere else? Make your own group with your own name, steps and labels.

Planned features

  • Name the group yourself
  • Choose your own steps and categories
  • Organize work that does not fit a default group

Diet Plans

Plan meals ahead of time, build grocery lists, and keep track of the eating plan you choose.

Planned features

  • Plan meals for the week
  • Turn a meal plan into a grocery list
  • Organize food and fitness goals together
