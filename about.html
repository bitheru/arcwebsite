About ARC

Less time organizing. More time doing.

ARC is a productivity website concept built around one idea: keeping your goals and daily tasks in the same place.

Why ARC?

School, work, personal projects and everyday responsibilities often end up scattered across different apps or notes. ARC aims to bring those plans together without making task management another task of its own.

What we're designing

The planned application includes task groups, shared workspaces, calendar connections and time tracking. Users would be able to divide larger goals into manageable steps and check their progress over time.

About this project

This website was created for CS146 in Fall 2026. The current deliverable focuses on semantic HTML, a consistent visual design, and responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and phone screens.
